ScoreTime – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tester Games ("we", "us") collects, uses and shares information when you play ScoreTime (the "Game") on iOS and Android.

The Game does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number or contacts. You play with an anonymous account and a player name you choose yourself.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Player name – the name you choose when you first open the Game. It is visible to other players on leaderboards and in friend search.
  • Friend requests and friends list – when you send, accept, decline or remove friend requests.

Information created while you play

  • Anonymous player ID – a random identifier created automatically so the Game can save your scores and friends. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Scores – your high scores, shown on global and friends leaderboards.
  • Game data stored on your device – high score, coins and settings (sound, music, vibration). This data stays on your device.

Information collected for advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS – only if you allow tracking),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, app version),
  • information about your interactions with ads.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Game's online features: your account, leaderboards, player names and friends.
  • To keep player names unique and to filter inappropriate names.
  • To show ads, including optional rewarded ads that let you continue a round.
  • To keep the Game working, fix problems and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Your choices about ads

  • European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: the Game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from Info → Privacy Settings in the Game.
  • iOS: the Game asks for permission before using your device's advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). You can change this in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • United States: depending on your state, you may have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. You can do this from Info → Privacy Settings when available.

If you decline personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your player name, scores and friends list for as long as your account exists. Leaderboards are reset periodically.

Delete your account in the Game: open Info → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your anonymous account, player name, leaderboard scores, friends and friend requests, and the game data stored on your device. Your player name becomes available to others again. This cannot be undone.

If you uninstall the Game without deleting your account, the data on your device is removed, but your online account data remains until you contact us to delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, use the in-game deletion option or contact us (section 10). Because your account is anonymous, we may ask for your player name to locate your data.

If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers and security

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, they use appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no online service can be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we may also notify you in the Game.
